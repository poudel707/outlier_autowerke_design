Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Outlier Autowerke respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when you visit this website or contact us about our automotive workshop services. Outlier Autowerke is based in Adelaide, South Australia.

1. Our obligations

This policy is intended to describe our approach under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), to the extent those laws apply to Outlier Autowerke. Some small businesses may be exempt from parts of the Privacy Act. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with the APPs regardless of whether an exemption applies.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • vehicle details, project requirements, service preferences and your message;
  • information you provide in an email, phone call, inspection or other inquiry; and
  • limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type and device information when this website or its third-party resources are loaded.

3. How we collect information

We collect information directly from you when you email us, call us, submit information through a working website form, request a quote, book a service or otherwise communicate with us. The contact form currently displayed on this website is a design prototype and does not submit information. If that changes, this policy and the form notice will be updated before information is collected.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling code from the Tailwind CDN. Those providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests those resources. We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools on this website.

4. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to inquiries and communicate with you about a vehicle or project;
  • prepare quotes, plan work and provide workshop services;
  • keep business, service and warranty records;
  • improve our website, services and customer experience; and
  • meet legal, insurance, accounting and safety obligations.

5. Disclosure of information

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to provide our services, including to contractors, suppliers, professional advisers, insurers, payment providers, technology providers and other service providers who assist our business. We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law, to prevent fraud or harm, or with your consent. We do not sell personal information.

6. Overseas disclosures

Some technology and communications providers we use, including providers of website resources or email services, may store or process information outside Australia. Where the APPs apply, we take reasonable steps required by those principles before disclosing personal information overseas.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

8. Access and correction

You may ask for access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. We will handle requests within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity. In some circumstances, the law allows or requires us to refuse access; if that happens, we will explain why where permitted.

9. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, contact us first using the details below. We will investigate and respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

10. Contact us

Privacy questions, access requests and complaints can be sent to hello@outlierautowerke.com.au.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new last-updated date.
