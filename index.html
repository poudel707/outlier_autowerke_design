WHAT WE DO

A small workshop that restores, modifies and builds — and documents every step.

Outlier Autowerke takes on restorations, modifications and one-off automotive projects for owners who want the work done properly, explained honestly, and photographed along the way. Every car that comes through the bay gets its own build sheet — from intake to final result.
