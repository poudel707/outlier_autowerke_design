About Outlier Autowerke

OUR STORY

It started with one car, a rented bay, and a refusal to hand off anything we couldn't explain.

Outlier Autowerke began as a single restoration project done on weekends, for the simple reason that nobody else would do the work the way it needed doing — patiently, honestly, and without cutting corners the owner would never see.

That project turned into a second, then a waiting list. What hasn't changed is the approach: every car gets its own build sheet, every decision gets explained, and nothing leaves the bay until it's right — not just finished.

Today the workshop takes on restorations, modifications and fabrication work for owners across Adelaide and beyond, alongside other shops and specialists when a project calls for it.
